# Godagent versus plain Terra crossed suite v1

## Purpose

Test whether receipt-bound Godskills improve `gpt-5.6-terra` across five materially different mission classes without changing model, reasoning effort, evidence access, or acceptance criteria.

## Conditions

- Every pair uses `gpt-5.6-terra` at high reasoning.
- Every pair receives the same mission and evidence boundary.
- The Godagent subject receives only the deterministic router-selected package.
- The control may inspect ordinary repository evidence but not binding files, Godskill bodies, capability contracts, or the paired result.
- Subjects have disjoint output paths and may not inspect one another.
- Mission and scoring files are committed before results are scored.
- Length alone earns no credit.

## Rounds

1. consequential implementation through `eternities-forge`;
2. reproducible failure diagnosis through `eternities-phoenix`;
3. exact repository research through `eternities-oracle`;
4. continuity reconstruction through `eternities-mnemosyne`;
5. visual-system direction through `eternities-muse`.

## Claim boundary

This suite can demonstrate consistent performance differences on these fixtures. It cannot establish universal model superiority, statistical significance, live-provider compatibility, or production safety. Any winner must still expose round-level losses and cost in time and output size.
