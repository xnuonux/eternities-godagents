# Godagent versus plain Terra crossed-suite evaluation

## Verdict

The receipt-bound Godagent condition wins **197 to 184 out of 200** across five matched Terra rounds.

- Godagent wins: implementation, debugging, and visual product direction.
- Plain Terra wins: continuity reconstruction.
- Tie: repository research.
- Godagent output: 6,577 words.
- Control output: 5,206 words.
- The Godagent advantage is real on this suite, but it costs about 26 percent more visible output.

## What the Godskills changed

The strongest effect was procedural reliability under consequential boundaries:

1. **Phoenix converted correct diagnosis into a truly executable artifact.** The control understood the bug but accepted a false-positive applicability check. Phoenix required a verified foundation, elimination ledger, evaluator-owned proof, and explicit rollback, and its patch survived the real gate.
2. **Forge improved evidence honesty.** Both implementations worked, but Forge accounted for the exact red state, write set, verification state, and non-integration boundary. The control produced stronger defensive object validation, proving that ordinary Terra can still outperform a Godskill-guided result on a local design detail.
3. **Oracle made provenance conflict visible.** It noticed that the architecture document still says “ready for implementation” even though current source and receipts show the implementation is complete. The control instead framed the broader readiness-label conflict more concisely.
4. **Mnemosyne did not automatically win.** Plain Terra produced the sharper operational handoff. This is useful evidence that a Godskill should not be treated as an unconditional quality multiplier.
5. **Muse turned taste into acceptance machinery.** Its strongest additions were not decorative prose but source classes, confidence labels, causal motion rules, static/reduced-motion fallbacks, performance limits, and rejection criteria.

## Integrity and limits

- Missions, bindings, fixtures, and rubric were committed at `4908cea` before subject outputs were scored.
- All five bindings were generated by the certified router and reproduced with exact release, source-envelope, route, stack, authority, and package digests.
- Both conditions used `gpt-5.6-terra` at high reasoning with identical mission evidence.
- Subjects had disjoint writes and could not inspect paired results.
- Both implementation artifacts and both debugging patches were preserved; disposable workspaces were removed afterward.
- All named Lunari evidence hashes remained unchanged.
- Exact task runtime telemetry was not exposed by the subagent interface, so no fabricated timing score is reported.
- Ten total samples remain too small for statistical significance. Task order was fixed rather than randomized, and results were scored by the coordinating agent rather than a blinded independent judge.
- This suite supports a bounded claim: on these five sealed missions, the Godskill condition was more reliable overall and decisively better on the recovery bug. It does not prove universal superiority or production safety.

## Next experimental gate

The next useful experiment is not another broad contest. It is a blinded replication of the debugging result with three new recovery defects and randomized A/B labels. That would test whether Phoenix's executable-proof advantage repeats, which is the strongest signal discovered here.
