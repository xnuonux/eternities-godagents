## certified boundary

The operator supplies one canonical identity-host policy and pins its digest
independently through `GODAGENT_IDENTITY_POLICY_SHA256`. The launcher accepts
that digest before it dereferences any policy-selected Godskills artifact. It
then verifies the admission tree and binding, exact admission-owned paths,
instance, Realm, transactional genesis, personal keel, local residency,
request identity, authority, routing context, and every artifact, token,
projection, cycle, process, and byte ceiling.

The host verifies the complete native descriptor, paired review and revision
executor descriptors, and exact Godskills routing executable. It constructs
the routing-evidence classifier from that verified executable and requires its
complete descriptor to equal policy. No caller-supplied classifier is accepted.

The historical admitted launcher now shares only the extracted tree, binding,
and policy-path checks. Its provider, credential, Realm, recovery, locking,
request, CLI, and result behavior remain unchanged and all prior launcher tests
pass.

## proof limits

This certification covers a programmatic host and injected descriptor-bound
transports. It does not qualify a concrete model, provider, endpoint,
credential flow, or output quality. It does not expose a CLI, migrate the
historical launcher default, provide hostile same-user isolation, or prove
universal exactly-once execution before observable output. It grants no Realm
effect, continuity admission, personal-keel write, identity evolution, Lunari
integration, Inspiration, or Soul activation.
