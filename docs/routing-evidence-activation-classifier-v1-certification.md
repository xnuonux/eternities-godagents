## verified behavior

- all twenty-two release-owned top-level routing cards are captured as frozen
  identity, family, and risk projections only after executable verification
- every current card maps deterministically into the closed activation task and
  consequence taxonomy
- mixed task classes collapse to `general` independent of selected order
- the highest selected verified risk determines consequence class
- mission prose cannot alter classification
- review availability, routing root, card digest, and taxonomy are bound in one
  authority-empty descriptor
- unknown, duplicate, malformed, or unverified selected evidence fails closed
- the classifier emits no activation mode or authority
- the real route selects `eternities-muse` for the certification mission
- the real activation executable selects deferred review from that classification

adoption, credentials, Realm action, continuity, keel writes, evolution, Lunari,
Inspiration, and Soul remain outside this certificate.
